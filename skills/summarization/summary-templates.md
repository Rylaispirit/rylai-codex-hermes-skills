# Summary Output Patterns

These compact templates belong to the Rylai Codex-Hermes-Claude workflow. Omit empty
sections rather than filling them with generic text.

## Snapshot

```markdown
**Bottom line:** [main point]

- [essential fact]
- [essential implication]
- [next action, if present]
```

## Executive Brief

```markdown
## Bottom Line
[Decision, conclusion, or recommendation stated by the source]

## Evidence
- [important figure or finding]
- [important figure or finding]

## Risks And Uncertainty
- [risk, assumption, conflict, or missing information]

## Decision Needed
[Decision and stated deadline, if any]

## Actions
| Owner | Action | Due |
|---|---|---|
| [stated owner] | [agreed action] | [stated date] |
```

## Technical Summary

```markdown
## Purpose And Scope
## Current Or Proposed Behavior
## Interfaces And Dependencies
## Key Decisions
## Trade-offs And Limits
## Breaking Changes
## Tests And Follow-up
```

## Meeting Summary

```markdown
## Decisions
## Actions
| Owner | Action | Due |
|---|---|---|
## Reasoning
## Disagreements
## Open Questions
```

## Research Digest

```markdown
## Question
## Method And Sample
## Findings
## Limitations
## Interpretation
## Practical Implications
```

## Multi-Source Synthesis

```markdown
## Shared Conclusions
## Source-Specific Findings
## Conflicts
| Topic | Source | Position Or Evidence |
|---|---|---|
## Gaps
## Combined Takeaway
```

Before release, compare the summary with the source for names, quantities, dates,
units, attribution, uncertainty, and negation.
