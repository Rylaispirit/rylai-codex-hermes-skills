# Academic Delivery And QA

> Portable Codex-Hermes-Claude replacement authored by Rylai.

## Rehearsal

Run at least three passes:

1. Structure pass: confirm the argument and transitions.
2. Timed pass: record time per section and cut excess material.
3. Delivery pass: practice emphasis, pauses, figure explanation, and slide control.

Do not solve an overlong talk by speaking faster.

## Q&A

Prepare concise answers for:

- Why this problem and not another?
- What is genuinely new?
- Which baseline or comparison is missing?
- What assumptions drive the result?
- What failure case matters most?
- How generalizable is the evidence?

Put detailed evidence in backup slides and know where each slide is located.

## Final Checks

- Fonts and symbols render correctly.
- Videos, animations, and links work offline when required.
- Citations are present and readable.
- The conclusion is the final non-appendix slide.
- Contact details are visible without a decorative "Thank You" slide.
- A PDF backup and required adapters are available for the venue.
