# Academic Section Patterns

> Portable Codex-Hermes replacement authored by Rylai.

These are rhetorical patterns, not claims to copy blindly. Replace brackets
with verified content and vary wording to preserve the author's voice.

## Abstrac

- Context: "[Task] is important because [specific consequence]."
- Gap: "Existing approaches remain limited by [verified limitation]."
- Method: "We introduce [method], which [mechanism]."
- Result: "Across [datasets/settings], [method] improves [metric] by [value]."
- Implication: "These results suggest [bounded conclusion]."

## Introduction

- "Despite progress in [area], [specific problem] remains unresolved."
- "Prior work primarily assumes [assumption], which may fail when [condition]."
- "We ask whether [research question]."
- "To address this question, we develop [approach]."
- "Our contributions are threefold: ..."

## Related Work

- "[Approach family] focuses on [goal], whereas [other family] emphasizes [goal]."
- "Our work builds on [line of work] but differs in [mechanism or evidence]."
- "The closest study is [citation]; the key distinction is [specific difference]."

## Method

- "The framework contains three stages: [A], [B], and [C]."
- "Given [input], the model first [operation], then [operation]."
- "We optimize [objective], where [symbol] denotes [definition]."

## Experiments

- "We evaluate the following question: [question]."
- "All methods use the same [data split/budget/protocol]."
- "[Method] improves [metric] from [baseline] to [result]."
- "The gain is consistent across [conditions], except [verified exception]."

## Limitations

- "The study does not establish [claim beyond evidence]."
- "Performance has not been evaluated under [condition]."
- "The observed effect may depend on [assumption or dataset property]."

## Conclusion

- "We presented [method or finding] for [task]."
- "The central result is [bounded result]."
- "Future work should test [specific unresolved issue]."

## Rebuttal

- "We thank the reviewer for identifying [issue]."
- "We agree that [valid concern] and will clarify [change]."
- "The requested experiment tests [question]; the result is [verified result]."
- "We respectfully disagree because [evidence-based reason]."
