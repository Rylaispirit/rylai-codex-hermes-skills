# Blog Writing

> Portable Codex-Hermes-Claude replacement authored by Rylai.

1. Define the reader, question, and useful outcome.
2. Choose one angle rather than covering the whole topic.
3. Open with a concrete tension, observation, or result.
4. Organize sections around reader questions.
5. Support factual claims with current sources when needed.
6. Use examples, counterexamples, and practical consequences.
7. End with a conclusion or action that follows from the article.

Avoid generic introductions, unsupported certainty, keyword stuffing, and a
conclusion that merely repeats headings.
