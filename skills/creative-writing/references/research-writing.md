# Research Writing

> Portable Codex-Hermes replacement authored by Rylai.

Define the research question, evidence standard, source boundary, and audience.
Separate observed facts, source claims, analysis, and inference. Maintain a
claim-to-source ledger while drafting.

Recommended sequence:

1. Question and scope
2. Background and definitions
3. Method or source-selection process
4. Findings
5. Contradictions and alternative explanations
6. Limitations
7. Conclusion and implications

Never fabricate citations, quotes, publication details, or numerical results.
