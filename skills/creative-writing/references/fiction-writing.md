# Fiction Writing

> Portable Codex-Hermes replacement authored by Rylai.

For each scene define:

- Point-of-view character
- Immediate objective
- Opposition or constrain
- New information or emotional change
- Ending beat that changes the next choice

Draft through concrete action, perception, and dialogue. Keep exposition tied to
what the viewpoint character notices or needs. Audit names, timeline, geography,
abilities, relationships, and established world rules before delivery.
