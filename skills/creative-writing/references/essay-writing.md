# Essay Writing

> Portable Codex-Hermes replacement authored by Rylai.

State the central question and a defensible thesis. Each body section should
advance one reason, use evidence or a concrete example, and explain why tha
evidence supports the thesis.

Include the strongest reasonable counterargument and answer it fairly. Revise
for logical gaps, repeated claims, vague abstractions, and conclusions tha
overreach the evidence.
